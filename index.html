Gestures

  • Pinch + drag — one hand — Pan
  • Open palm, move — one hand — Rotate
  • Two hands, apart/together — Zoom
  • Fist — one hand — Reset view
  • Thumbs up / down — Wireframe on/off

Snap to view

Count with one hand (thumb side first, then pinky side), press a number key, or click a button above.

  • 1 — Thumb only — Front (same pose as thumbs up — that gesture wins; use the key/button for Front)
  • 2 — Thumb + index — Back
  • 3 — Thumb + index + middle — Left
  • 4 — Thumb + index + middle + ring — Right
  • 5 — Pinky only — Top
  • 6 — Pinky + ring — Bottom
  • 7 — Pinky + ring + middle — Iso

Mouse also works any time: drag to rotate, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Or click a button above.
